Rotations fitted to the existing MPFB rig; travel removed, stride loops and
foot phase aligned. No CMU subject model or tool code is distributed.

The native skinning, other fitted motions and pigmentation come from the local
Human - Animation Comparison.blend audition, SHA256:
0946d797f3c620492444f2ec65d2a88280053fbda2c882dde3dfaf826942d229

Runtime derivatives omit subdivision, bake unlit material colour/opacity and
use camera-only local-body hiding. This prototype has no final wardrobe, facial
performance, character creator or complete interaction/animation coverage.
