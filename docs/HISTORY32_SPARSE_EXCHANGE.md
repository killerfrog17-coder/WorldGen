# Sparse inhabited exchange — guarded realm18 experiment

This is an experiment, not a claim that European settlement or worldwide
agricultural diffusion is solved. Existing realm17 saves retain the literal
preceding rules unless explicitly upgraded.

## Recorded failure

The independent frozen Q17 Earth replay reproduced its original179800 endpoint
exactly (`30b1bca54ca8f374b1ff0b044fcec53d6ab5d572f9fe957ec7a4102fff4f5210`).
All80 detached observations preserved their publication hashes and final public
validation passed. A fixed accepted French field (cell176034,49.46N1.81E) had
64–77 regional people throughout the observed171000–179800 interval, no teacher
and no received exchange. Regional contact previously required100 residents.
The hard return made that inhabited district an absorbing barrier. This does
not establish that every neighboring route otherwise carried a viable crop,
or explain all of Europe: the separately sampled Sanala and Mesikani regions
remained above100 contact residents and had substantial exchange histories.

A different northern French community, Sanala269, had no positive-fit dated
crop candidate until the178900 observation (stored receipt178897), despite
earlier generic agricultural contacts. Its foundation was172561. Present-day
clearing and adoption rates cannot explain six millennia before any eligible
crop donor appeared. Its current clearing multiplier0.935 would reduce the
remaining conditional practice time by only about6.5% if removed entirely.

## Narrow change

Existing bounded regional exchanges may meet in a smaller inhabited district.
The encounter factor is `sqrt(min(1, residents/100))`, quantized to one million.
It is zero for no actual residents and exactly one at100 or more. Both source
and destination staging populations use the existing aggregate contact-resident
estimate, including actual administered rural people; no census or new
population account is introduced.

The product multiplies the existing regional encounter **hazard**, before its
`1-exp(-hazard)` probability conversion. Paid town-to-district missions use the
recipient factor on their existing single-visit success probability. Physical
routes, dated receipt, local terrain/latitude costs, transport range, food,
staffing and treasury constraints remain. A remembered teacher in an empty
district cannot provide a living carrier.

The square-root shape is an authored encounter prior, not an empirically fitted
archaeological rate. It removes a discontinuity; it does not make sparse places
more connected than dense ones or guarantee successful transfer. Cultural
recognition, agriculture, institutional retention and state formation keep
their separate conditions. No crop, farming practice, town or state is granted.

## Important separate limitation

Current regional technology packages are bitmasks, not inventories of named
crop lineages. A non-farming intermediary can retain knowledge of agriculture
without retaining its teacher's seed phenotype. This is a real representational
limitation, but a proposed direct seed-profile fix failed the actual Q17
counterfactual: better admissible alternative donors already existed in the
positive-fit cases. It has not been implemented merely because it sounds
plausible. The sparse-route and exact temporal audits remain distinct from that
unproven explanation.

## Research and review

[The2024 primary Neolithic dispersal study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51335-4)
models farmer migration and admixture along different inland/coastal routes.
[The2020 climate/archaeology study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-020-0897-7)
finds substantial regional slowdowns, rather than a uniform wave of conversion.
These support testing migration, exchange and ecological continuity separately;
neither validates this game's exact square-root encounter coefficient.

Pure tests cover zero carriers, sparse positive encounters, symmetry,
boundedness, monotonicity and unchanged factors above the old threshold.
An actual savedQ17 French-route counterfactual passes26 assertions. With the
actual71 recipient residents, both existing gate-passing land routes deliver
under18 but never under17. Only route selection is controlled. Dense100/100
endpoints produce exactly matching normalized full-state hashes in both rules,
including an actual successful delivery. Empty-recipient controls fail; the
zero-source control is used only where removing the real regional pool does
not leave an additional actual administered cohort. No people/cultures/towns/
states are created and the original save hash stays unchanged.
