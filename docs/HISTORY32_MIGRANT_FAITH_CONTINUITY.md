## Measured causal problem

The actual AA26 final Earth evidence (`153e69ae…`, year157700) contains459
recorded religions, of which379 have the inherited-migrant description. Yet its
archive records only two schisms and eight syntheses. This is not itself a desired
or forbidden count: source inspection establishes the error. Every newly named
migrant culture allocated a new religion, then copied the **parent culture's
default** faith profile into that new ID. A departing town's actual public faith
and remembered household rites could differ from that default. Cultural naming
therefore manufactured religious fragmentation and could also lose a real adopted
tradition. The52% root-family town share was genealogical continuity, not proof of
one continuing public faith.

This repair does not prescribe how many large religions should exist. The actual
AA26 faith56 already spans191 towns, three cultures and48 courts through existing
contact, patronage and household mechanisms. Those mechanisms and real renewal
conditions remain; migration alone is not an institutional schism.

## Historical comparison, not a universal sequence

The British Library's [International Dunhuang Programme collection account](https://idp.bl.uk/discover/learning/buddhism-on-the-silk-roads/articles/transmission-of-buddhism/buddhist-travellers-and-pilgrims/)
connects actual travel, oral teaching, translated texts, merchants and locally
supported monasteries. Its manuscript letters request assistance for a pilgrim
from particular officials and monastic communities. These are concrete carriers
and institutions, not a rule that crossing a language boundary necessarily creates
a separate religion, nor that incoming travellers instantly convert residents.

The Met's [Gandharan objects and inscribed patronage](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/gandhara)
illustrate inherited religious practice alongside locally varied artistic forms
and actual local endowment. Stylistic variation, different languages and political
succession are not interchangeable with a new doctrinal lineage. The sources do
not calibrate the model's numerical conversion/support rates or imply that all
traditions follow Buddhism's particular history.

## Bounded prospective contract

`FoundMigrantAggregateCulture` is used only after an existing production caller
has actually transferred people or established a populated funded post. It reads
one supplied live source town when that town provides migrants, otherwise the
explicit physical departure cell. It reads that community's public tradition and
remembered household tradition separately. A paid lay minority is **not** assumed
to represent every emigrating family. If the rural endpoint has no named public
faith, the already-debited migrant cohort's culture default is an explicit,
flagged proxy; no distant homeland, ruler's capital or contact teacher is queried.

Before the founder overwrites its cell, the wrapper records destination rites.
A named incumbent with preceding residents retains both public and remembered
household identity, existing institution and review history. The incoming remembered
household tradition can enter the single unfunded visitor slot, subject to the
existing recent-carrier priority. Repeated same-identity arrivals preserve the
first-contact date but cannot renew a historic paid missionary's strength for free.
An actually empty destination inherits source public and household IDs, starts
its own local review clock and receives zero institution, pressure and dispute.
It receives no ready church, food donation, treasury, sovereignty or membership
census. Existing faith vocation, reach, name and founding metadata are unchanged.

The same guarded continuity also applies to an actual parented town founding.
That caller has already written its culture default into the public layer, so
the helper must use the explicitly passed **pre-transfer** public-faith snapshot,
not mistake the just-written incoming default for indigenous practice. Existing
household memory, institution, dissent and review date remain intact for residents.

The founder's original reflection signature is preserved. Its distinct internal
helper accepts an inherited ID, so a descendant culture does **not** allocate an
orphan placeholder faith or emit a fictional new sect. Native/contact recognition
continues to use the original founder, with no inherited-faith argument. A parent's
language or cultural relationship alone is not evidence of migrant religion.

`AppendLanguage` also needs a29-only ownership check: naming a new culture may
rename its own newly founded faith, but must not rename a shared source religion.
