# Regional heritage before advanced culture - optional realm36

This addresses a real omission: appearance already evolved in regional ancestry
accounts, but named identities depended on advanced-culture actors. Foragers were
therefore visually/inspectably anonymous even after long geographic separation.

Fresh36 histories and explicit upgrades use identity-extension Version2. A living
regional community can have Culture=-1: no advanced-culture actor is required.
The founding band receives an identity at Start. Existing region clocks ensure
occupied communities have one; actual household migration carries the departing
regional identity and bounded resident minority shares. This changes no population,
technology, route, culture-origin gate or sovereign territory account.

Peripheral communities more than350km from their recorded identity homeland can
differentiate after at least6000 years of sustained local separation/attachment,
with an elapsed-time hazard rather than a common deadline. Same-identity local
traffic does not permanently suppress differentiation across the whole dispersed
founder population. Parents and up to four compiled social origins remain saved.
Those numbers are authored gameplay priors, not fitted ethnogenesis rates. New
recognition does not imply that all people in a patch suddenly thought alike.

The regional account and individual town accounts stay separate. Town migration,
language, faith, mixed residence, inclusion and ordinary identity continuity keep
their existing rules. Explicit upgrade preserves old identities and physical
traits; currently occupied but unrecorded regions receive prospective identities,
not fabricated ancient genealogies. Version1 and realm35 saves remain literal
until the owner's explicit historical-rules upgrade.

## Physical appearance / future NPC contract

Social identity and biological appearance remain separate. The existing saved
eight regional traits evolve through inheritance, actual migration, drift and
slow UV/body-form response, including before advanced cultures. The Atlas now
exposes these in ethnic-community articles rather than only culture/city articles.
A homeland sample is not a global average of everyone sharing an ethnic name.

`Prehistory.SampleNpcAppearance(state, cell, individualSeed)` deterministically
samples a bounded individual from the regional distribution in fixed8-trait work.
It returns skin/hair/iris pigmentation and colors, hair curl, body compactness,
two neutral facial/nasal model axes and stature potential. Profiles overlap;
individuals are not identical copies of an ethnic template. A future persistent
NPC should store its sampled appearance at birth/spawn rather than resampling
every frame or after a political identity changes. This is a parameter foundation,
not a full facial genetic model, an inherited individual pedigree, calibrated
human morphometrics, or a completed skinned NPC mesh/character creator.

Illustrative portraits deliberately show several sampled individuals. Their
simple shapes are schematic, not reconstructions of actual people. No appearance
trait changes intelligence, skill, morality, culture-origin probability or tech.
[Smithsonian's account of UV and skin variation](https://humanorigins.si.edu/evidence/genetics/human-skin-color-variation)
supports the exposure mechanism;
[AABA's statement](https://bioanth.org/about/aaba-statement-on-race-racism/)
supports continuous, overlapping variation and separation from racial boxes.
Neither source calibrates these game parameters. We do not assign Earth's
ethnicities, faces or traits by continent names.

No new simulation queue or daily population census is added. Normal work is one
existing regional update or an actual bounded migration. Explicit upgrades,
validation, actor snapshots and on-demand Atlas exports retain their documented
actor-enumeration costs. There is no new fine-cell or territorial-membership pass.
