## Bounded correction

Raw `AutonomyPressure` remains. Only its territorial-secession interpretation
gets continuity credit from ACTUALLY installed government bureaucracy, local
inclusion, participation, financing and the existing physical travel distance.
A live territorial service node AND a physically allowed capital-to-town route
are required. A rich distant capital with radio alone is insufficient.
Partial service funding yields partial credit; zero funding yields none.
Actual fiscal crises, conquest, war losses and the separate claimant/succession
path remain. Non-bureaucratic states receive no blanket continuity bonus.

The rule also corrects government maritime travel to use the RULING culture's
transport capabilities instead of the LOCAL population's capabilities. This
is appropriate for government reach, not a change to local migration or seed
adaptation. The independent final-W22 audit finds only one affected distance
case, with zero old/new risk, so this asymmetry is NOT claimed as the dominant
cause of that replay's fragmentation.

Where secession really occurs, an existing recent same-culture regional
successor may receive the town instead of minting another flag. This checks
only the existing at-most12 physical town neighbors,50-year founding window,
actual travel distance and allowed route. It creates no population or free
provisions and performs no empire/region/culture cell census.
