# Territorial continuity versus local autonomy: candidate23

This prospective history rule does not erase dissent, change cultures, impose
a number of countries, or upgrade existing22 saves automatically.

## Observed failure

The complete V21 Earth replay ends with1,156 living courts under1,021 actual
sovereigns. The largest population sovereign holds less than1% of the total
population under states. This is not merely a palette or vassal-count issue.
Conversely, median living-court age is136.5 years and only2.94% exceed1,000
years: a blanket claim that all states are immortal is not supported.

The ordinary town-politics path treats requests for autonomy, exclusion,
printing-era political demands and fiscal discontent as territorial secession.
It always creates a new court rather than using the bounded regional successor
coalescence already present in the separate disputed-succession path.

An independent read-only audit of complete W22 Earth finds4,171 owned towns,
of which1,814 meet ordinary age/population eligibility. Applying the proposed
formula to their CURRENT recorded conditions reduces1,121 current hazards;
299 fall below the territorial threshold. Summed current hazards move6.69625
to3.97904. These are counterfactual current risks, not observed future events,
historical back-projections or a fitted target country count. Eighteen unserved
towns retain their positive risk unchanged. A recently ruled foreign town
with inclusion125 and demands.998 remains high-risk (.07609 to.07248).

## Bounded correction

Raw `AutonomyPressure` remains. Only its territorial-secession interpretation
gets continuity credit from ACTUALLY installed government bureaucracy, local
inclusion, participation, financing and the existing physical travel distance.
A live territorial service node AND a physically allowed capital-to-town route
are required. A rich distant capital with radio alone is insufficient.
Partial service funding yields partial credit; zero funding yields none.
Actual fiscal crises, conquest, war losses and the separate claimant/succession
path remain. Non-bureaucratic states receive no blanket continuity bonus.

The rule also corrects government maritime travel to use the RULING culture's
transport capabilities instead of the LOCAL population's capabilities. This
is appropriate for government reach, not a change to local migration or seed
adaptation. The independent final-W22 audit finds only one affected distance
case, with zero old/new risk, so this asymmetry is NOT claimed as the dominant
cause of that replay's fragmentation.

Where secession really occurs, an existing recent same-culture regional
successor may receive the town instead of minting another flag. This checks
only the existing at-most12 physical town neighbors,50-year founding window,
actual travel distance and allowed route. It creates no population or free
provisions and performs no empire/region/culture cell census.

## Interpretation and acceptance

[Dincecco's original comparative fiscal study](https://users.nber.org/~confer/2007/eges07/dincecco.pdf)
studies European fiscal centralization and constraints on government rather
than equating every local privilege with state independence. It does not
establish a universal modern stability coefficient. The simulation's.45
pressure-credit amplitude is an authored, reversible game prior. Ancient
conquest and modern excluded colonies must remain genuine counterexamples.

Seventy pure guard/boundary assertions pass. Actual private-caller,
conservation, cold continuation, old22 literal continuation and complete fresh
Earth/Continents Plus era reviews remain separate acceptance work. A positive
current counterfactual is not proof of realistic consolidation or colonization.
