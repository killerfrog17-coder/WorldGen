# History32: local succession coalitions, revision6

Experimental source work, 17 October 2026. Fresh and explicitly upgraded
realm6 histories receive nullable `HistoricalRealmLifecycles.Claimants`.
Existing realm1–5 saves retain their earlier behavior and exact absent-field
canonical representation. This is not a packaged-player acceptance claim.

The causal audit and historical comparisons are in
[the realm episode report](HISTORY32_REALM_EPISODES_AUDIT.md). It demonstrated
genuine rapid fragmentation and country endings already in fresh E histories,
while distinguishing old country identities from unchanged sovereignty or
dynasties. Revision6 addresses narrower missing links, not country expiry.

## Personal conquest

A successfully transferred inhabited town credits the responsible current
leader with one recorded capture victory and5,15 or25 renown according to its
resident scale. Empty terrain does not. New renown stops accruing at1000;
existing larger historical values are not reduced. The existing physical
campaign, supplied force, marching/siege costs and conquest-debt accounting
still determine whether a capture happens. A reputation cannot buy soldiers,
food, movement or territory. Existing succession pressure can consequently
recognize a real conqueror's personal record even when sieges met no opposing
field army of twenty men.

## Local political memory

Only a real disputed central succession can seed a new claimant. The center
must be an existing supported noncapital town, at least fifty years old with
at least600 residents, adequate stability and no active demographic distress.
Actual central exposure bounds the initial hazard; a long preceding interval
between city checks is not backdated as years of succession conflict. Local
inclusion, centralization and recent conquest affect emergence. No ethnicity,
religion, biome or country age grants a revolt quota.

A sparse persistent tree keyed by city ID locates one latest/recent record per
participating city. The record saves the named representative, support, actual
center/owner, declaration and review years, originating crisis, ordinary
leadership turnover and eventual outcome/successor. Earlier named episodes
remain in the existing archive; no inactive record is allocated for every city
or cell. The representative has a finite ordinary tenure: a continuing claim
can receive new local leadership without implying an immortal individual.

The coalition advances only after its city's existing political-calendar gate.
Support depends on local inclusion, viable institutions, recent incorporation,
actual receipts versus services, public works, distance, representation and
war exhaustion. Restored inclusion and reliable service can reconcile it;
loss of the resource base reduces support rapidly. Actual loss/abandonment of
the center resolves its influence, and a changed owner cannot inherit a
phantom hostile territorial claim. Merely moving the capital to the town does
not automatically reconcile its local opposition.

A supported local claim can outlast the8–25-year central crisis. Its saved
support supplies shock to that city's existing regional-separation process.
Physical neighboring successor courts still permit coalescence. A genuinely
new court inherits its locally recorded claimant; joining an existing court
does not overwrite that court's ruler. Both outcomes are narrated and saved.
The existing founding path divides actual treasury and army stocks. Claimant
support itself creates no money, recruits, people, independent country or
separate field army.

## Inspectability and limitations

Country profiles now say **Last contested court change**, with "None recorded
since foundation" where appropriate. Ordinary officeholders and current
sovereignty remain separate. Country/city articles and a clicked state or
territory's city card expose the local claimant and support. On-demand atlas
queries display at most twelve recent claimant centers, never terrain holdings.
Support is an organizational indicator, not a vote count or population share.

This is deliberately not a full civil-war model. It does not add mobile
claimant camps, independent fiscal escrows or competing army units. Automatic
legacy capital relocation, continuous historical territory/peak-date records
and sparse independent state origins remain separate concerns. A central
claim can reconcile, separate or join a neighbor; no universal breakup date,
mandatory dynasty cycle or target number of dead empires is imposed.

## Validation

`--realm-claimants-only` exercises emergence constraints, persistent support,
reconciliation, resource loss, sparse copies, literal null-field canonical
compatibility and town versus empty-land reputation. The initial suite passed
eleven checks. `--realm-claimants-world WORLD` additionally exercises existing
saved centers, conserved actual successor founding, named inheritance,
displacement, whole-world save/load and cold12-year versus5+7-year replay.
The final eighteen-check world suites passed both E Earth5 and baseline CP20.
They use the actual regional-contest path after the central deadline, retaining
real physical neighbors, rather than substituting a direct founding call.
They additionally verify finite local leadership turnover and reconciliation
before an ongoing central crisis ends. The optional-canonical suite passed208
checks with the newly nullable fields.
