## Actual cause and bounded change

`AdvanceAggregateRegion` cannot create an agricultural learning actor until an
inhabited, anonymous region develops a recognized local tradition.
`FoundAggregateCulture` allocates its agricultural practice records and schedules
the culture actor; a regional teacher/knowledge receipt alone does not run those
crop-adoption processes. The preceding recognition equation discounts contacted
communities by the same static wild-resource/soil concentration factor used for
pristine opportunities, even after substantial physical exchange has accumulated.

The actual AA26 final India region1457 has11,044 raw residents, teacher14,
exposure1000, a dated receipt26years old, delivered Storage knowledge and strong
local civic example. Its existing recognition probability is only
.0018083369017483086 per100actor-years. This corresponds to about55,249years at
the *current constant hazard*, not a forecast or reconstruction of its past.
The squared-resource ecological concentration is.021797329; its diffusion
multiplier `.02+.98*ecological` is.04136138242. Environmental cultural adoption
still contributes a separate approximately.657447 discount. Thus static
wild-surplus concentration, not that second discount alone, binds this case.

Revision28 substitutes1 for **only** that diffusion ecological multiplier when
all of the following existing saved conditions hold:

- valid regional teacher and accumulated exposure>=600;
- a positive dated exchange receipt, neither future-dated nor older than250years;
- at least400 actual raw regional residents, not an apportioned Remote estimate;
- actual delivered regional Storage knowledge;
- `InstitutionalRegionalExample`>=600 from the current actual regional court,
  falling back to the remembered civic teacher only where no actual court exists.

The current regional court uses `CachedProvincialCohort`: at most five existing
actual cell-controller entries and their population-cohort resolution. It does
not use recognized territorial titles, enumerate a country's territory, or prove
that every household in the large region has the same affiliation. This priority
matches the existing physical regional-exchange path. A remembered original
court is provenance, not a requirement for a permanent hotline to that court.

The original independent/pristine hazard remains literal. Concentration,
environmental cultural adaptation, cultural maturity, the400-resident caller
gate, arrival/cooldown/site checks and named random stream remain. There is no
additional eightfold multiplier. Revisions0–27 retain their old diffusion
expression and do not execute the new qualification query.

No new recurring process, auxiliary saved counter, RNG stream, cell census or
climate regeneration was added. Exposure and the latest receipt establish
accumulated contact evidence; they are **not** an exact saved count of distinct
visits. This conservative civic-qualified repair also does not accelerate all
forager-to-forager exchange lacking civic examples.
