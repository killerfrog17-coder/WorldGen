# Crop species niches — guarded realm19 repair

Existing18 and earlier continuations keep the preceding ecological arithmetic.
This changes historical cropping, not the accepted climate/terrain or the
classification algorithm. It has no Earth coordinates, biome whitelist or date
gate. Full-world geographical review remains required.

## Actual failure, not a hypothetical missing seed

The independent frozenQ17 Earth replay with80 detached probes twice reproduced
the original final hash exactly. Northern France's Sanala community had
agricultural contacts with cultures14 and65 for millennia, but their only
adopted crop was **potato**. Their actual imported potato fit was exactly zero
in France and Sanala. The source profiles were not absent, and their existence
must not be confused with a missing dated crop donor.

The code chose the potato-specific cool-season window correctly, but then
multiplied potato physiology by the generic tropical-root-garden suitability.
That generic factor is zero at a growing-window temperature of15C or lower
and additionally treats winter cold as if the crop were a tropical perennial.
France's recorded six-month window is13.3C; Sanala's14.6C. Both can have a
substantially warmer frost-free summer. Positive potato-specific physiology
was therefore erased by the wrong plant-group envelope.

## Narrow correction

Only potato (catalog10) receives a cool-season structural niche instead of
the tropical-root-group niche. Actual cultivar maturity, moisture requirements,
photoperiod, growing-season length and permafrost remain. Slope, salinity,
soil fertility, leaching, forest cover and the preceding abiotic physical gate
remain. The existing potato reproductive heat-stress factor remains.

The authored lower-temperature ramp spans7–15C. Existing reproductive stress
provides the hot end; no new universal temperature bonus is introduced. Crop
selection, native availability, transport, dated contact, adoption work and
paid fields are separate and unchanged. The six-month calendar is deliberately
unchanged in this isolated experiment; whether it should reflect individual
crop maturity is a separate question.

The fit cache now includes the species-rule flag. A reader explicitly comparing
old and new branches on the same engine cannot reuse an old cached result as if
it were a new ecological calculation. No recurring census is added.

## Research grounding

[FAO Ecocrop's potato sheet](https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/dataSheet?id=1971)
lists a broad7–30C temperature range and90–160-day crop cycle.
[FAO's agronomic grouping](https://www.fao.org/4/x5648e/x5648e0e.htm)
places potato with temperate C3 crops, separately from cassava/yams.
These support correcting the class mismatch; the game's normalized ramps are
authored priors, not a calibrated potato-yield model or a claim of historical
crop distribution on Earth.

Fresh19 Earth review has substantially different agricultural geography. Its
early population and long interval from the first proto-centers to states are
being independently criticized; a positive local counterfactual alone is not
evidence of a satisfactory whole-world simulation.
