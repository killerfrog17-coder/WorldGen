# History32: continuing countries and contested realms

Work-in-progress opt-in review extension `Connections.Realms`, version1.
Generation3.32, accepted climate, relief and ecology do not change. Fresh32
histories opt in. Existing32 saves require `UpgradeRealmLifecycles`; absent
records retain the exact preceding continuation and canonical representation.

## Defects identified

The aggregate dispatcher used `TryLivingRegimeChange`, which ended a polity,
created another with the same name and transferred its entire persistent
territory/population root. Its founding year changed even without territorial
change. The older `ContestHistoricalSuccession` territorial breakup routine was
not the active aggregate path. Aggregate legitimacy recovered toward900 whenever
cash remained, while conquest debt decayed even during a contested succession.
Those are mechanisms for stable boundaries with misleading apparent turnover.

## Current experiment

Government changes retain state IDs, treasury, inhabitants, diplomacy, territory,
and original foundation. An independent date records contested regime changes.
Explicit migration reconstructs continuity only from recorded diplomatic
continuity successors; it does not merge conquered or seceded states.

Successions evaluate recent conquest debt, elite capture, exhaustion, geographical
service load, personal military authority, actual deployed writing/roads/telegraph,
public works and political representation. Multi-center realms can enter a saved
8–25-year contest. Existing supported regional centers and constituent governments
then act on their own calendars. Compatible nearby successor courts can gather
other centers through actual bounded city contacts. There is no simultaneous
world/realm/culture cell census or arbitrary birthday-based empire expiry.
Regional territory changes reuse the existing bounded provincial transaction;
populations and treasury are not copied into multiple successor states.

Local administration distance and centralization now contribute to ordinary
provincial pressure. Legitimate authority responds toward fiscal/institutional
support instead of automatically rising indefinitely with a positive treasury.
Cash-funded relief remains separately effective. Contested succession prevents
the ordinary automatic conquest-debt reduction from erasing the unresolved shock.
Fresh early states still require the existing ecological/provisioning conditions;
this is not a relaxation of independent civilization origins.

The active diplomacy path now addresses at most two previously contacted courts,
not an omniscient state list or mostly-own-city random peers. Supplied competitive
neighbors have consequential campaign opportunities; allied courts and modern
international restraint remain meaningful. Conquest takes actual theaters and
field supplies. Representative and modern impersonal administration damp personal
succession shocks; improved communications also help ordinary cohesion. Mass
political exclusion separately raises foreign constituent independence pressure,
including when a colonial capital's local culture differs from its ruling court.
Regional secession divides existing treasury and levy resources rather than
giving a daughter court another independent copy of its parent's funds.

## Recognition and geographical review

Recognition can address bounded adjacent physically reachable districts from an
actual mature capital or funded live office, once appropriate road/ocean or rail
capability exists. An informed peaceful trading partner and sustainable funds
are required. Recognized area and annual diplomatic service obligations update
by transaction deltas. Dead holders do not reserve titles forever; a later valid
agreement can replace that extinct title without duplicating the regional index.
Legal sovereignty neither creates settlers nor removes indigenous cultures or
foreign governments. Its annual upkeep is part of the state's real service bill.

Exploration previously substituted one regional representative beach for actual
departure and landfall geometry. A large coastal region could span opposite
sides of a continent. Realm experiments add geometry-only bearings from the
actual port:64 possible headings, at most96 fixed raster samples per query,
and an ordered16,384-entry derived FIFO cache. It retains the shoreline lookup
already calculated during historical setup. No target-resource oracle or fine
terrain enumeration is added to an ordinary event. Costs, preparation, physical
route checks, arrival failures and returning reports remain. Arrival reconnaissance
stays around the actual landing instead of jumping to the representative beach.
Predecessor null-realm histories retain their original candidate selection.

## Historical comparisons and limits

- [Metropolitan Museum: Hellenistic kingdoms](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/art-of-the-hellenistic-age-and-the-hellenistic-tradition): rapid conquest, division among successor courts, later smaller separations, and continuing cultural institutions motivate separating sovereignty from cultural extinction.
- [Cambridge History: united Mongol empire1206–1260](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-the-mongol-empire/rise-of-chinggis-khan-and-the-united-empire-12061260/40F64848E13817221B45FB39EA4EC88E): civil war in1260 did not erase the shared Eurasian communicative space. Independent constituent governments must preserve knowledge, people and connections.
- [British Museum, Room41 guide](https://www.britishmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2021-05/large_print_guide_room_41.pdf): Roman administrative division and persistent eastern institutions motivate differentiated institutional continuity, not a universal one-step collapse.
- [United Nations: decolonization](https://www.un.org/en/global-issues/decolonization): self-determination and external legitimacy matter independently of metropolitan military capacity. There must not be a predetermined20th-century unlock or mandatory political outcome.

These are design comparisons, not statistically fitted historical laws. The
pure contrasts and save/load/partitioned/cold-cache-world checks are necessary but do
not establish convincing global history. The first 500-year modern CP review
produced a real large-realm contraction, but the first early 5,000-year reviews
still had too little regional turnover. Further iterations and screenshots are
required before selecting this player over the preceding geographic review.

## Follow-up mechanisms and measured review

Ruler tenures now span3–50 years with a mean around19, rather than18–50
years for every court. Government replacement does not reset the country date.
Fiscal failure of a multi-center court opens a regional contest instead of
simultaneously deleting every local government. Modern impersonal administration
reduces the generic court-failure hazard, not military defeat or local opposition.
Long-established overseas settler governments can seek self-government even when
they share the metropolitan ruling culture. Actual tribute, distance, local
institutions and external threats affect that pressure.

Recognized districts retain a witnessed service source and accumulated physical
land-route effort. Existing source nodes must still be live, including actual
delegated constituent administration. A geometry-only land-route index is built
at setup; a treaty review considers no more than eight adjoining land districts.
It no longer wastes that budget on duplicated sea connections. Modern ministries
can negotiate while cohesion is middling; neither insolvency nor an extinct
administrative source becomes effective authority merely through technology.

Actual colonial patrons may fund a treasury grant sufficient for a regional
administration's reserved obligations. Money moves between existing accounts.
An actual patron can recognize a constituent's charter; siblings cannot manufacture
external recognition for one another. Established independent trading courts
retain their separate recognition role. These rules do not manufacture settlers,
crop suitability, political access, local consent or indigenous cultural erasure.

Legal jurisdiction follows its recorded administrative center, rather than
forever retaining the old imperial holder or disappearing after a court's death.
An optional persistent source/document index queues at most eight legal
transactions per local review. It neither enumerates empire cells nor loops over
all of its titles in one event. Old entries are checked against the current
witnessed source, so a reassigned service base is not taken over through a stale
index entry. Publication copies detach the allocation counter. Absent optional
indexes preserve old canonical bytes and require an explicit upgrade to enable.

Historical contrast: [Berlin Act1885, Articles34–35](https://worldjpn.net/documents/texts/docs/18850226.T1EX.html)
distinguished notification and effective authority from a bare declaration.
This is a comparison for inter-state recognition mechanisms, not endorsement of
colonial claims or a claim that imperial recognition established indigenous consent.

Outstanding: early-era geographic spread and technology pacing remain too slow
in these Earth continuations, modern northern-interior coverage is incomplete,
and natural conquest/fragmentation frequencies are not historically calibrated.
Do not hide these failures by changing era labels, assigning arbitrary states
to blank regions, or declaring the controlled succession scenario a successful
ordinary historical run.
