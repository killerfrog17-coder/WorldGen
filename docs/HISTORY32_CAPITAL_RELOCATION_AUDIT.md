# History32: capital loss and government relocation audit

Read-only diagnosis after the F/realm6 freeze, 17 October2026. No frozen F
physics has been changed. The first bounded sample does **not** establish
systematic impossible court flight, and does not justify killing every country
when its capital falls.

## Method and first observations

`--capital-transitions-audit WORLD YEARS NEW_OUTPUT` advances a private saved
state for1–100 years without upgrading it. Annual actor/contact observations
record changed capitals and real government endings, relevant dated archive
events, national resources, and actual surviving center alternatives. It
validates the endpoint and checks that the input hash is unchanged. This is an
explicit offline actor/contact-graph inspection, never a recurring simulation
country/terrain census. It does not write a replacement world.

The first sample continues the fresh-E CP Medieval episode endpoint185650
under its saved realm5 rules, through185750. Output is
`D:/WorldGenBuildArtifacts/overnight-20261017/capital-transitions-e-cp-medieval-100/transitions.json`.
There are12 annual capital-change/end-state observations: **seven actual
government endings and five surviving relocations**, all associated with
explicit military captures. No abandonment, local separation or service
withdrawal caused a capital transition in this particular sample.

National receipts/service/public works are reported separately from local
stocks. They are not measured city office budgets. Annual treasury or army
deltas include normal economics and fighting; they cannot be attributed
entirely to relocation. Food reserve is existing storage, not a new estimate
of actual annual rations consumed. Earlier cities may change hands more than
once within an annual observation, so this is not an exhaustive transaction
log.

## What the active code does and does not do

`AssignAggregateTown` removes a lost center and selects
`remainingCities.At(0)` immediately. This neither ranks loyal/provisioned
refuges nor requires a route, funds a move, records relocation, or independently
starts a political crisis. In four observed relocations legitimacy and
cohesion are numerically unchanged across the annual observation, despite
loss of the capital. That is a narrower demonstrated gap than universal
immortality.

However, the military operation saves its **original** opponent capital.
`TryRevisedMilitarySettlement` tests ownership of that original core and
accumulated campaign pressure. Choosing a new capital does not erase defeat;
the Dylponbotdym example demonstrates actual submission. Likewise,
territorial sources remain actual local city/office records. Capital selection
does not itself repaint all holdings or create inhabitants. Captured sources
enter the existing gradual service-recovery process.

The treasury and public-works indicators are country aggregates. They do not
identify a capital vault or locate every state asset in the lost city. It
would be equally unsupported to confiscate all national money or erase all
public works automatically. Repeating that shortcut would recreate the
capacity-collapse problems documented in the urban lifecycle audit. Individual
rulers also lack physical location: their survival or capture cannot simply
be inferred from a capital flag.

## Historical constraints

The [Met's curatorial account of Southern Song court art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/40052)
distinguishes the1127 capture of the northern capital and members of its royal
family from Gaozong's escaped southern court, established at Lin'an in1138.
Continuing dynastic claims did not mean the same captured household silently
reappeared at an arbitrary city; patronage also asserted the new court's
legitimacy.

The [Met's Byzantine synthesis](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/byzantium-ca-330-1453)
describes1204 as fragmentation into competing provincial states in exile,
not one unaffected country moving its capital. Nicaea's later recovery of
Constantinople under a new dynasty combined continuity claims with changed
territorial and political power.

[Madison's actual1814 letter, preserved by the U.S. Capitol and National Archives](https://www.visitthecapitol.gov/artifact/president-james-madisons-letter-senate-regarding-temporary-accommodations-congress),
records concrete emergency accommodation for Congress after the Capitol's
destruction. Existing institutions could continue nearby without the country
ending. The [Senate historian's account](https://www.senate.gov/about/historic-buildings-spaces/past-chambers/emergency-quarters.htm)
also describes actual borrowing and rebuilding decisions. These are mechanism
contrasts, not universal ancient-versus-modern survival probabilities.

## Focused proposal for a later explicit revision

F remains frozen. A subsequent output-changing rule needs a new marker/explicit
upgrade and exact realm1–6/null-extension continuation controls. On present
evidence, the narrow first step should be **bounded supported refuge choice,
honest relocation records and differentiated disruption**, not a new automatic
collapse rule:

1. At a real capital-loss transaction, inspect at most a small fixed number
   of existing owned centers and existing contact/office links. Rank by
   supported population/institutions, local inclusion, food and route effort;
   use explicit ordered ties. No country-cell census, remote-culture default
   or ancestry walk. Reuse surviving local governments instead of inventing
   people or ready-built infrastructure.
2. Distinguish military overrun, provisioned planned movement and loss of an
   unviable settlement. Use actual available transport and food/pay capacity;
   a marine movement must share the finite transport ledger, not obtain a free
   fleet. Charge any explicit relocation expenditure once against actual
   existing public funds and record the cost. Do not assume the whole
   treasury was physically in the old capital. A future capital-vault subset
   would have to be funded from, not added to, that treasury.
3. Personal court defeat should cause temporary authority/succession pressure
   according to actual integration and the surviving administration. A
   functioning bureaucracy with nearby funded offices can preserve continuity;
   rival supported centers may negotiate or separate through their existing
   local calendars. Do not reset national public works/medicine to zero or
   automatically restore local claimant loyalty when the capital arrives.
4. If no adequate refuge is demonstrated, represent disputed/displaced
   central authority separately from surviving local government. Do not call
   the existing last-center ending routine on a whole multicenter country:
   its population, local stores and remote cohorts must not vanish or be
   relabeled en masse. A longer multi-year court-transit model would require
   its own saved state and careful central-service consumers; it should not
   be smuggled into a small selection fix without evidence and review.

Controls should include this sample's credible nearby refuges, a more capable
nearer alternative, a cut or unaffordable overseas leg, hostile local claimants,
a single-center defeat, emergency continuity with surviving offices, and a
failed move with surviving civilian communities. Verify food/money/army
balances, no double-charge on replay, save/partition equivalence, cold-route
equivalence and exact frozen F continuation. No Core implementation is
authorized by this diagnosis itself.
