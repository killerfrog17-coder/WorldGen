## Revision11 mechanism

Navigation grade and observation date no longer conflict. A newer physically
delivered witnessed account (grade2) can replace obsolete host/shore/opportunity
facts while retaining an existing grade3 survey. Older stronger navigation may
improve grade without overwriting newer observed facts; hearsay cannot erase
witnessed facts. Copies preserve the original observation year, never delivery
year. Same-date facts do not oscillate with arrival order. Sharing retains the
existing bounded peer/candidate budgets, actual trading connection, report
capacity, queue and travel clock. It sends changed host/shore or opportunity
changes of at least100 points, rather than fresh-date heartbeats alone.

Known own offices must have an actual currently preparable farmer/station plan
before another settlement convoy is purchased. These checks use the existing
source community, household, field, spacing, food, investment and finite craft
quote. Foreign-state missions first require their own court's actual political
readiness, available troops, money, provisions and transport. Predeparture
selection does not consult live foreign armies. An unusable aim on a report
at least15years old may instead justify reconnaissance; this is not an extra
settlement waiting period. Unknown exploration retains its existing portfolio.

At actual foreign landfall the obsolete fixed600-cohesion plus1.5x-technology
outer veto is removed. The inner government readiness remains, including
leader ambition and exhaustion. Comparative force is quoted using unreserved
troops, real local supply, treasury, field range and shared finite hull credit.
Equal technical capability is not by itself a veto when a genuine material
advantage exists. A successful declaration funds the ordinary campaign: troops
must subsequently be provisioned/reserved, march, fight, suffer losses and
achieve their political aim. A landing does not instantly subordinate the
whole foreign government. Negotiated independent-town councils remain a
separate existing path; factories leased under a sovereign host remain absent.

## Historical constraints and limitations

Columbus's1493 printed report explicitly supported another voyage; the Spanish
edition was followed within a month by a Latin edition circulating in Europe.
This supports prompt physically carried copying, not exclusive century-long
knowledge or an instantaneous global hotline. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/treasures/tr00.html)

The East India Company's New Year's Gift journal records Lizard9March1614,
Saldanha15June, Madagascar6August and Socotra9September, followed by operations
at Surat and Bantam. The existing several-month voyage scale is therefore not
the measured multi-decade project bottleneck. Port permission, local trade and
war are different outcomes, not interchangeable colonial colors.
[British Library voyage journal, via Qatar Digital Library](https://www.qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100083800017.0x000018)

Cortés's self-interested letters describe the1519–21 conquest campaign,
including defeat and renewed operations. Rapid political expansion is possible
but cannot be modeled as an uncontested remote chart annexation.
[Original letters and collection context, Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/resource/gdcwdl.wdl_19994/?sp=1&st=gallery)

The finite cargo-day accounts are authored aggregate priors, not reconstructed
ship tonnage. Current observation years have integer precision; same-year
contradictory reports intentionally retain the first accepted facts. This
change does not correct the accepted world's warm Antarctic climate, invent
sea ice on warm routes, or block independent oral community voyaging. Native
performance and matched long-window improvements require separate measurement.
