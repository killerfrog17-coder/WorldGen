SERIAL GPU WORKER
  python scripts/run-fauna-overnight.py --watch
It resumes saved stages and runs only animals with prepared references and motion
banks. worker.lock prevents duplicate producers; verify PID before resuming a
disconnected worker. queue-state.json has current animal/stage and failures.
Create batch PAUSE to finish current safe subprocess/candidate and stop. Do not
kill shared ComfyUI or owner apps.8GiB free-space guard stops new work. Each ready
audition keeps four low-poly model/rig candidates and one review; only its exact
raw high-poly/latent/prepared-reference intermediates are removed after reload.
References/prompts are from built-in imagegen, not game-painted textures.
After all motion-backed candidates have run, the same queue prepares four raw
meshes and neural rigs for source-gap animals. These remain separately labelled
models_rigged_motion_pending, with raw-motion-pending.json and no animated
Blender-review claim. They do not count as completed auditions and cannot be
painted yet. Adding a suitable motion bank later resumes assembly from saved
raw candidates. No reference/model work is thrown away for a source gap.
run-control.json mode=pause also requests a safe stop. An earlier completed
internal PAUSE marker is retained as a record; mode=run plus the explicit
ignore_completed_internal_checkpoint flag ignores only its exact original text.
An owner-created/new PAUSE always stops the worker. Never delete this marker to
work around an approval rejection. Verify worker.lock before restarting.
