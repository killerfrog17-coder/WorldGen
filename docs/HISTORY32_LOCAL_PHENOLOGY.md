# History32 local crop phenology (guarded revision17 experiment)

This is a causal experiment, not a validated historical replay or a released
player. Revision16 and earlier retain their existing behavior.

## Observed failure

The independent M14 Earth field audit found that a real Moroccan wheat holder
had existed for337 years but accumulated only30 local practice years. The
selection clock multiplied elapsed years by normalized crop yield. Inherited
selection years could improve yield traits without making an imported seed's
photoperiod response appropriate for its actual new fields. Ordinary forest
preparation alone was insufficient for that actual cultivar.

## Narrow change

Revision17 separates **phenology selection generations** from the preceding
yield-weighted practice clock. Annual groups use one successful growing season
per year; the perennial group uses one generation per20 years. Movement toward
the actual field latitude is probability-rounded at an authored prior of0.3
tenths of a degree per generation, capped at the local target. This means about
333 successful annual generations for a10-degree mismatch, not instant universal
adaptation. These coefficients are scenario priors, not a measured universal
genetic rate.

Eligibility requires an actually occupied, supported, cultivated town, its own
local seed inventory, a recent crop-use survey listing that exact crop, at least
ten actual planted farmer equivalents, and reproductive fit of at least0.08.
Acquisition dates bound elapsed time; failed reviews still advance the review
date, so recovery cannot collect centuries of fictitious past selection.
Stored, unused, unsupported, abandoned and import-only lots cannot improve.

The preceding weighted clock still controls physiological/yield traits and
husbandry years. No temperature, moisture, soil, origin prior, physical seed
delivery, food stock, clearing cost or population constraint is waived. There
is no recurring cell census. The existing city event examines at most six
seed groups and its bounded local crop survey.

This remains a coarse local-variety-selection model. Annual tuber cropping is
not literally annual sexual reproduction, and selecting useful standing
variation is not the same as waiting for a new mutation. Those distinctions
need richer crop-specific treatment if this model is expanded.

## Research grounding and limits

[FAO's wheat physiology chapter](https://www.fao.org/4/y4011e/y4011e06.htm)
describes quantitative photoperiod responses and genotype-dependent
vernalization. It does not justify replacing all crop climatic requirements
with a single latitude tolerance.

[Teixeira et al.'s tropical maize selection experiment](https://www.nature.com/articles/hdy201490)
shows that recurrent selection can change flowering time substantially within
ten generations. Its five admixed accessions and large selected populations
are not evidence for a universal25-degree migration in ten generations: the
18–43-degree interval describes evaluation sites. This experiment supports
distinguishing reproductive generations from normalized harvest volume; it
does not calibrate the simulation's specific movement prior.
