# Local town-to-hinterland delivery

This history-32 candidate repairs a specific missing physical contact route. A
minority migrant town can share a regional aggregate with many rural residents,
yet neither the regional majority teacher nor a search for nearby *towns* will
necessarily select that actual donor. Existing direct-contact receipts are
already consumed without rerouting through the original culture homeland.

On the existing forty-year city-contact opportunity, a supported town with at
least 1,000 urban residents can try its own regional anchor and at most two
existing neighboring land-route anchors. There is no region-wide city search,
membership census, new scheduled actor, or ancestry walk. Actual current
overland routing, barriers, transport, foreign-state trade access and resident
population are checked again at delivery.

The modeled outreach party is 12–60 people, derived from current town size. Its
round-trip provisions are removed from the actual donor warehouse; sponsored
missions additionally charge the actual government's treasury and require
positive service coverage. This is a workforce-and-resource proxy, not an
explicit ledger reserving individually employed workers. Unsponsored supported
towns pay provisions without receiving a fictitious state subsidy.

A successful visit records dated actual donor knowledge and local institutional
exposure. It does not award technology levels, adopted crops, farmers, cities,
states or population. Existing learning, actual donor cultivar availability and
recipient crop compatibility still govern subsequent uptake. The source
culture's existing aggregate knowledge remains a model approximation; this is
not an individually educated emissary simulation.

## Validation and limitations

Six pure checks and seventeen actual-world integration checks passed on each of
the saved Earth-200k and Continents-200k controls. They cover real positive
deliveries, one-time food/fiscal debits, no direct population/technology/crop
grant, missing provisions/funding, abandoned or unsupported donors, foreign
hosts without trade, preserved legacy rules, serialization and partitioned
continuation. Positive targets were selected from actual saved terrain and
accounts, not constructed permissive ecology.

A matched 500-year continuation of the same S2 Earth save was compared using
actual states and technology screenshots and detached regional audits. East
Asia changed from two live towns / 6,176 urban residents to three / 7,593. A
native recipient gained Roads and Mechanized Agriculture *knowledge* through
normal receipt uptake, but still lacked Writing and retained its root-only crop
repertoire. India had no towns in either continuation; its very dry accepted
climate is an independently identified upstream limitation.

This is a local causal repair, **not evidence that late global urbanization or
technology diffusion is realistic**. Both screenshot controls still show a
severely under-urbanized Asian interior in the observer Postmodern era. Physical
seed stocks, agriculture, sustainable local production and eventual deployed
institutions remain distinct requirements. The matched outputs are generated
artifacts under `regional-delivery-ab`, not source-controlled fixtures.
