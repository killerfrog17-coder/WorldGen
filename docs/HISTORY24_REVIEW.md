# History 24 experiment: finite research and constituent governments

This is an in-progress implementation checkpoint, not a release acceptance report. The installed desktop shortcut still opens the previous history-23 build. Terrain, climate, ecology and generator versions are unchanged.

## Structural changes

The old loop independently funded every eligible technical capability. History 24 replaces it with one finite specialist-effort budget split across concurrent projects. Capabilities have repeatable levels and level-specific prerequisites. Separate contact-delivered learning accounts are cheaper than independent investigation. Demand follows sampled real communities, neighboring competitors, war pressure, recent disease, cultivation and coastal access. Iron/steel are dual-use crafts; isolated peaceful societies do not receive an automatic gunpowder-research incentive.

Known technical levels, next-level work, current allocation and installed productive capacity are separate persisted records. Real transport, military, medical and food effects read deployment rather than assuming that hearing a recipe builds an industry. The atlas exposes levels, allocations, progress and deployment. These remain declared game-model priors, not estimated research expenditure or measured household equipment coverage.

Constituent governments retain local courts, culture, cities, finances and territorial accounts while paying an overarching government. An imposed settlement or voluntary protection compact can create allegiance; weakened protection, fiscal arrears, succession and local power can undo it. Local fiscal actors forward bounded ancestor deltas rather than scanning subjects or terrain. Succession roots preserve allegiance without visiting every constituent. States maps show imperial entities; Territories maps expose local governments. Geography affects contact, field supply and administration. The legacy globally scanned dependency process is not enabled.

## Early experiments and rejection criteria

The first finite-budget candidate was much too slow: Earth did not reach early-modern capability until more than ten millennia after its first state. It was rejected as a pacing result. The third candidate still took about 8,667 years to attain every capability on 200k Earth. Its screenshot sequence also retained excessive small-state patchwork. Those pictures are diagnostic failures, not release evidence.

The current fourth/fifth candidate increases finite research productivity without restoring per-project free effort. Preliminary fresh 200k results: Earth first state 106665 and all 37 knowledge capabilities 112347 (5,682 years later); random Continents Plus seed 261015 first state 107203 and all capabilities 112629 (5,426 years later). This is knowledge, not universal deployment. Longitudinal images, contact gaps, regional deployment, constituent behavior and native performance still need review before acceptance.

Reproduce focused invariants with `--technical-only <history23-world>`: 97 assertions currently cover the ordered leveled graph, increasing level costs, explicit migration, conserved budgets, blocked unsupported research, knowledge/deployment distinction, local/imperial map identity, cycle rejection, nested cached totals, independence, saved records and publication-partition equivalence. The canonical-byte suite also passes. Old rules remain isolated behind their recorded version and nullable extensions.

Research context: [Galimulator developer description](https://snoddasmannen.itch.io/galimulator), [technology adoption evidence and distinctions](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr169.html), [extensive versus intensive adoption](https://www.nber.org/papers/w16379), and [the Met's Amarna letter from the ruler of Tyre](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/544696). These motivate mechanisms; they do not supply the numerical coefficients. No external implementation was copied. Galimulator's public description supports expansion, changing political priorities and internal strife, not a claim to have inspected its proprietary algorithms.

## Work still in progress

Verify the native UI and 200k late-game throughput. Review practical knowledge transmission and provincial/imperial fragmentation. Preserve old-save continuation and keep source checkpoints on GitHub. Do not call this release-ready merely because it compiles or reaches the final technology.
