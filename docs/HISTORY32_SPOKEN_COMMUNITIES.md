# Spoken communities and religious plurality

History32 optional realm32 extends the existing language30 naming foundation.
Generation, geography, observer eras and music are unchanged. Old saves keep
their literal rules/hashes until the explicit historical-rules upgrade.

## What is represented

`History.Languages.Spoken` saves independently named speech varieties, bounded
sound profiles, parent provenance, culture defaults and sparse regional/local
community records. Naming profiles are not reinterpreted as speaker censuses.
One language can cross cultural/state borders; a culture can contain several
languages. Existing proper names and recorded history are never regenerated.
New located names can use local speech and its reusable roots.

Each community records a main home language, one home-language minority share,
one learned second language and overlapping competence, plus administrative
and ritual domains. Home shares total1,000; second-language competence is not
added to them. Two largest represented home communities are an approximation,
not an exhaustive multilingual census. Unrecorded primitive speech is unknown,
not evidence that foragers have no language. Upgrading an old world initializes
prospective approximations, not a reconstruction of its unrecorded minorities.

Actual paid/route-eligible contact delivers language exposure. Shared religion,
ancestry, a remembered teacher or a distant ruler alone does not provide it.
Trade/contact builds bilingual competence, with easier uptake of related
speech and gradual loss of unused second-language competence. Visitors do not
create resident minorities. Actual town founding migrants carry their physical
departure community's speech; recorded resident/migrant quantities determine
the retained home share without adding people to demographic accounts.
Uncounted cultural-foundation contact is not falsely assigned a migrant ratio.

A supported local office with writing can favor its administrative language.
Home-language replacement requires existing competence and gradual
intergenerational shift; a flag change is not instant language replacement.
Maintained ritual institutions can preserve a separate ritual language. Real
contact, administrative or ritual domains reduce isolation. Prolonged local
separation can append a related variety, inheriting roots and composing regular
sound changes without mutating every speaker of the parent language. Repeated
contact can contribute bounded phoneme-adapted loans. No recurring ancestry
walk, world/culture/state/ecoregion census, or new simulation clock is added.
Existing region/city/contact events do fixed-size local record work. Explicit
upgrades, integrity checks, snapshot actor copies and atlas/renderer exports may
enumerate actors; renderer exports may enumerate cells as before.

Structural intelligibility compares compiled sounds and basic word order. It
is graded, separate from bilingual competence, and only a procedural proxy.
Local language barriers reduce existing physically delivered regional learning
opportunities without an absolute language wall. There are no climate-to-sound
stereotypes or language-based civilizational aptitude bonuses.

## Viewing and religious depth

**View → Languages** (hotkey8) colors the main local home language. Selection,
borders, legends and atlas links use language identity, not a state or culture
substitute. The language atlas gives sounds, roots, related varieties and town
examples. City articles show the local home/second/admin/ritual distinction.

Religion already saves more than one faith per location: public predominance,
inherited household tradition, a separately provisioned lay association,
visiting teachings and two maintained institutional bodies. Each denomination
has its own inherited doctrinal profile/branch issue; institutional affiliation
can differ without a new doctrine. These are local records, so a province's
towns need not share one denomination or the court's religion. Local inclusion,
funded exclusion and particular disagreements affect adoption and grievance;
denominational difference alone is not an inevitable conflict.

The new bounded `ReligiousPresenceAt` projection distinguishes actual resident
evidence from recent visitors. Cards expose different household/association
traditions; city and institution articles explain the overlap and link the
denominations. There is intentionally no invented percentage-of-believers
derived from an influence score, nor an assertion that every congregation is
represented: at most two institutional bodies and one lay association per town
remain the explicit limitation. The religion map is public predominance, not
uniform belief or a complete population census.

## Evidence basis and limitations

[UNESCO's language vitality framework](https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/00120-EN.pdf)
separates intergenerational transmission, domains and institutional treatment.
[Max Planck's bilingual development discussion](https://www.mpi.nl/blog/two-languages-one-mind-how-bilingual-childrens-languages-influence-each-other)
motivates overlapping competence and cross-language influence, not automatic
replacement by the politically dominant language. Neither source calibrates
the game's rates. All numerical rates/thresholds are authored scenario priors.
This is not full linguistic reconstruction, generated scripture, TTS, complete
grammar, school-policy simulation or a measured census of language/religion.
