## Consequence

These observations do **not** justify an automatic culture assignment to an
entire administered region, a blanket first-town threshold reduction, a wetland
or aridity waiver, or a shorter town-expansion age. No Core physics was changed.
Further causal work needs a complete physically funded positive counterfactual,
or dated actual rejection evidence, rather than a good teacher/global cultivar
fit or a large apportioned contact denominator. Current endpoints, funds, seed
acquisition dates and the19-year-old town cannot explain an earlier era without
that additional temporal evidence.
