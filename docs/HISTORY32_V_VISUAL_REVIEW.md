## Second-agent cross-check

An independent ecology reviewer (not the port/outreach implementation author)
also actually inspected Pangeae states at 134000/141500 and religion at 143100,
Ice age states at 445000/457000, and the large replay outputs:

- Earth: `history32-v-earth200-replay/year-129600/{states,technology}.png`.

Their independent conclusion agrees with the limits above: Continents Plus has
real urban chains on every major landmass and multiple political footprints;
Earth remains heavily North-America-centered with sparse European/Chinese
urbanization. India's false aridity must not be hidden by granting crops or
courts. The novel worlds' first courts are localized favorable coastal sites,
but screenshots alone do not causally explain the very long Ice age
agriculture-to-court interval. The next review is a frozen-calendar opportunity
and research-gate audit, not a declaration that V is completely realistic.
