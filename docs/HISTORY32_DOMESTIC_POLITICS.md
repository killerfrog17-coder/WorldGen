# Enduring domestic power - optional realm37

Realm37 replaces the preceding freshly derived five-interest political view with
saved organized interests and governing coalitions. It does not change world
generation, technology costs, eras, music, sovereign colors or population units.
Fresh histories use it; existing saves remain literal until an explicit rules
upgrade. Upgrade registers current institutions prospectively, without inventing
ancient factions, elections or coups.

## Organizations, not labels painted onto cells

Each government has five bounded institutional seats: landholding/provincial
notables, trading/craft interests, officers, religious institutions and community
spokespeople. These are potentially overlapping sources of leverage, not five
exclusive social classes, a membership census or modern parties in every era.
Each retains its recorded foundation, meeting center, available actual notable
representative, influence, organization, grievance, constitutional preferences and
spending priority. A government retains its coalition and dated decision record.
Bodies survive individual rulers and their representatives' deaths.

Actual urban/rural accounts, practiced commerce, troops, funded religious
institutions and participation/printing condition their gradually changing
organizational capacity and influence. Influence reconciles to1,000, but does
not purport to measure electoral votes. Existing public specialists can become
representatives; an unnamed body does not fabricate a famous politician.
Town loss causes a bounded replacement-seat choice, not a national census.

## Material bargaining and consequences

The next assembly is chosen in a named4–12-year window, serviced by the existing
government actor clock. It does not become a new daily queue or synchronized
worldwide political tick. An assembly costs actual treasury units; unsuccessful
negotiation still costs money, and insolvency defers it. Political organization,
existing allies, constitutional affinities and ruler diplomacy condition coalition
formation and acceptance. An agreement moves practice toward its partners'
preferences in bounded steps, rather than automatically making every government
more representative forever.

Coalition partners steer the existing conserved defense/works/relief/learning
budget; opposition retains some influence. This reallocates paid spending, not
free troops, food or scientific work. Exclusion, policy mismatch, provisioning
failure, war strain and religious grievance condition opposition organization.
Mobilized opposition can reduce receipts by at most15%, intensify succession
contests, and contribute to already-local, resource-conditioned claimant politics
when central legitimacy has actually failed. Different ethnicity alone is not a
rebellion switch. Neither a dissatisfied group nor a changed coalition transfers
land automatically.

Representative succession can select a real available public figure from the
governing coalition. Dynastic households retain their existing heir/regency path.
A distinct armed office contest requires an excluded, highly organized and
aggrieved officer circle, a living adult commander not campaigning elsewhere,
actual troops, severe legitimacy failure, opposition support and treasury funds.
It replaces the office holder with that existing person, paying organization
costs; it neither kills the former ruler nor creates a new army/country. Provincial
institutions may dispute the resulting court through the existing sovereignty
and military systems. A25-year cooldown and continuous hazard prevent a general
leader-change lottery. These are authored scenario priors, not fitted coup rates.

## Atlas and cost contract

State articles expose **Domestic power groups** and **Institutional bargaining**
under **Power & relations**, alongside the existing actual spending program.
Bars show influence; rows distinguish coalition/opposition and give organization
and grievance. Links open recorded representatives or their actual meeting place.
The decision, payment and political continuity are inspectable without a text
dump. Routine bargaining stays out of global headlines; major officer transfers
can appear. All dated events remain in the archive.

`Realms.Domestic` is optional and absent from old canonical bytes. Two immutable
sparse trees hold12 fields per government and12 per institutional seat. One
government update handles exactly five groups and bounded existing seats, never
all cells or all members of an empire/culture/ecoregion. Scalar opposition reads
do not build profiles. Copying shares immutable pages; ordinary hashing uses
tree digests. Explicit migration, integrity validation, saving and on-demand
Atlas directories retain their usual actor-enumeration exceptions.

## Historical grounding and limits

[UK Parliament's history of representation](https://erskinemay.parliament.uk/section/4500/history-)
describes nobles, churchmen, knights and burgesses acquiring negotiated voice.
[Its account of financial authority](https://erskinemay.parliament.uk/section/5682/financial-relations-between-the-crown-and-parliament)
connects fiscal dependence to bargaining over supply. These motivate material
institutional leverage and continuity, not a claim that all societies follow
England's constitutional history or that these coefficients are empirical.

This is a bounded internal-power foundation: not individual voting, a complete
electoral/party-formation simulator, private household wealth accounting, a
countrywide class census, tactical capital fighting, or a guarantee of calibrated
all-era political outcomes. It builds on, rather than replaces, actual city food,
treasury, army, religion, identity, claimant and sovereignty mechanisms. Full
late-world realism and the outstanding200k/max-tech+1000 speed criterion remain
separate acceptance questions.
