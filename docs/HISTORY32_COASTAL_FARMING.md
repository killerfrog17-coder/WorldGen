# History32 W: real local farming from provisioned harbors

This is an explicit, nullable experimental extension to V's paid maritime harbors. It changes neither generation nor the carried-crop suitability rules. Frozen V and older saves retain their null-policy continuation until `UpgradeCoastalFarming` is explicitly requested. Fresh current-history setup enables the empty policy.

## Actual reason for the change

The V observer maps are not a global-modern pass. The Continents Plus northwestern diagnostic box (15–65°N, 145–40°W) contains 10,387 land cells / 26.49 million km², but only 40 live towns: 34 ordinary farming centers and six provisioned ports. Those towns contain 1.96 million urban residents and 82,586 explicitly allocated rural residents; only two are active suppliers. Only 510 cells have state ownership. Regional colors or a few coastline markers are not evidence that this productive-looking interior is developed.

The read-only V audit found 87 live Earth provisioned ports, 45 with a nominal ordinary local-settlement rate, and two current three-probe targets where a copied normal farm transaction would succeed. Continents Plus had 93 / 52 / five. But **all seven** ordinary copied transactions drew national remote people rather than using only actual residents at the harbor. Example: the northwestern port 1952 has 3,515 urban residents. The old ordinary transaction wanted 2,250 emigrants, only 628 of whom could come from its actual local urban surplus. Removing the suppression without changing the household transaction would have reintroduced a global teleportation loophole.

Separate physical limitations remain. A harbor with no viable carried crops nearby does not become a farming colony because its ruler has money, radios or an unrelated language. The saved Earth's overly dry India is still an upstream abiotic issue, not justification for giving immigrants unrecorded rice.

## Bounded, cohort-sized establishment

The new source is a healthy, supported, owned provisioned harbor with actual recent food delivery, funded public works, usable stock and treasury. Its local cohort is `min(1000, floor((urban population − 1000) / 4))`; at least 200 actual people are needed. This leaves the harbor's retained urban residents in place. No regional rural or national remote population is substituted.

The existing opportunity uses the same three spatial proposals, the same annual hazard formula and the same physical/adaptation tests. Only the inappropriate 1,600-transferable-household minimum is replaced for this special actual urban cohort. A successful new site must still have:

- The source community's own actual carried, viable crops and cultivation practice.
- A reachable same-landmass location, ordinary terrain/water/season/lake constraints, free site, spacing and regional land availability.
- Actual service, recognized-claim and political access conditions, current construction money and real startup/travel provisions.

The cohort becomes about one fifth urban and four fifths rural residents. A 200-person start is therefore a fragile 40-urban / 160-rural farm community, not an instant civilization. Normal subsequent growth, disease, local insecurity and abandonment still operate.

Reserved catchment area is `ceil(cohort × 0.1 / local crop yield)`, bounded to 50–2,000 km². This is a regional farm catchment, **not a claim that every hectare is immediately cleared**. Actual land commitment and forager opportunity are debited through the ordinary regional ledger. Existing terrain/time travel costs apply, plus a construction/clearing charge with a fixed 500-account-unit floor and a proportional area/cohort component. Preflight and commit revalidate the same current household, field, stock, route and fiscal quantities; failed attempts do not charge any account.

## Food and family provenance

Source urban residents are debited once. The same people become actual child urban/rural households, and world population is conserved. Startup food for the whole cohort and additional travel rations are debited once. As in ordinary farming foundations, the urban founders' rations enter the named warehouse; rural first-growing-season rations are consumed establishing the fields. Therefore aggregate named warehouse stocks fall by **travel plus rural establishment consumption**, not travel alone. The founding event makes that distinction explicit.

`Connections.CoastalFarming` contains `SinceYear` and an immutable sparse `Families[childTown] = parentTown` policy index. Queries address that index directly; they never walk ancestry, scan cultures or census regions. Children and subsequent descendants use their actual local urban/suburban/rural household budgets for further founding and cannot draw national remote residents. Their normal modern city events also cannot pull an unlocated national population into an isolated overseas village.

These descendants do not receive analytic `CommunityUrbanImports` from a global national farming ledger. They rely on their actual local farm production and genuinely debited neighboring cargo. Otherwise a tiny overseas farm could become a virtual national pantry and feed its parent harbor from food that was never shipped. Other ordinary farming towns and null-policy saves retain their existing rules.

The parent harbor retains its import-dependent basis. A real younger farming community can eventually become a separately validated paid supplier under the independent W supplier-routing feature; simply creating the village does not guarantee that it can feed a metropolis. Permanently local-only family accounting is deliberately conservative: later integration of distant state rural pools requires another physical/dated migration model, not removal of these guards.

## Focused transaction evidence

Current-source tests pass129 pure checks plus28 actual Earth integration checks and31 Continents Plus integration checks, including the additional northwest-specific fixture:

- Earth port 188 → cell 85602: 1,000 actual urban emigrants become 200 urban / 800 rural, reserve a 936 km² catchment, consume 108 additional travel rations and pay 3,091 investment units.
- Continents Plus port 1408 → cell 24293: 654 actual people become 130 urban / 524 rural, reserve 334 km², consume 41 travel rations and pay 1,164 investment units.

The tests cover exact person/food/fiscal/area accounting; failed stock, funds, household, delivery, works, health and ownership preflights; local-only descendant transfers and actual city upkeep; immutable policy copies; partitioned execution; real snapshot roundtrip and continued hashes. Raising a copied state's national remote and farm-potential accounts to `10^15` does not increase the child's food capacity. No source crop gate was loosened.
