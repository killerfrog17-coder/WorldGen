# Peoples, belonging and local historical memory

Optional history32 realm33 builds on the separate spoken-language and religious
systems. It does not change generation, appearance, ancestry demographics,
era labels or music. Older histories retain their literal rules until explicit
upgrade. The extension is nullable `HistoryState.Identities`, saved/hashed and
omitted from old canonical bytes when absent.

## Identity is not a copied map

A named people records an origin community, homeland, recognition year, founding
language/religious context and inherited social traditions. Identity is separate
from culture classification, citizenship, language, religion and biological
ancestry. No racial category or intelligence/technological-aptitude score is
added. Existing culture/state/city names are preserved.

Initialization uses the already recorded local/cultural continuity, not an
invented prehistoric ethnic census. Multiple cultural actors in one home region
can retain one heritage. Physical descendants carry source-community identity;
mere teaching contact does not transmit people. Upgrading an old save creates
prospective approximations and does not fabricate its earlier ethnic history.

Sparse regional and local records retain the main identity and one resident
minority share. Actual resident/migrant quantities combine up to four bounded
components; two leading identities remain, with smaller unrepresented groups
folded into the main coarse estimate. This changes no population ledger.
Visiting outsiders create contact, not a resident minority. Neither conquered
territory nor a renamed culture automatically changes household identity.

Communities maintain cohesion, homeland attachment, actual contact history,
separation and sustained mixed-community experience. Supported mixed residence,
inclusion and bilingual interaction can permit a new shared identity over
generations; intermarriage openness conditions the pace. A long-separated local
community with differentiated speech can sometimes develop a distinct branch.
Neither event is forced by language or religion alone. New identities are local:
they do not recast all members of the parent group, change borders or mint states.
Parents remain archived. A maximum four-origin compiled **social** inheritance
summary provides bounded graded relationship queries; it is emphatically not
DNA, a biological ancestry model or a measure of purity.

Ruling identity is stored separately per government. A conquered/relocated
capital's resident population does not suddenly redefine the conquering court.
New local courts use their founding community; constitutional continuations
inherit their court marker. This is one coarse court identity, not a census of
every official or a complete simulation of elite assimilation.

## Local memory and political consequences

Each participating town retains its latest previous court, recorded local
governing tradition, established rights, inclusive experience, attributed harm
and one salient other-town relationship. Siege hooks record actual capture
violence and the attacking court/center. Routine administrative transfers do
not invent atrocities. Changes of government do not erase earned privileges;
constitutional continuations do not reset them. Previous episodes remain in
the existing dated archive rather than being copied into an unbounded new log.

Actual exchange can build a relationship; negative memories can survive a
unifying empire and then fade through generations or renewed contact. This is
a bounded local memory, not an all-pairs opinion matrix. Fair treatment permits
grievance to decay faster; no permanent inherited hatred is assumed.

City inclusion, local autonomy pressure, nearby successor compatibility,
disconnected-court protection and constituent-government foreignness now use
local identity versus the **court's recorded heritage** when the extension is
active. Related groups have graded social affinity; identical culture labels
are no longer the sole political identity test. Ethnic difference alone creates
no new grievance under fully inclusive representative rule. Exclusion, lost
rights and actual attributed violence matter; coethnics can resent those too.
Existing finance, route, crisis, viable local government and campaign gates
remain. Identity never grants a border, forces union or directly starts a war.
No predetermined Earth-like national borders or ethnic-world partition is used.

## Necessary language-account corrections

New33 rules let supported resident language minorities sustain daily practice
without needing a foreign visitor every generation. Relatedness changes learning
**speed**, not an impossible permanent proficiency ceiling for unrelated tongues.
Frozen32 retains its previous arithmetic. Regional language/identity clocks
write their regional account even if its representative point contains a town;
they cannot overwrite that town's separate migrant/minority record.

## Inspection, cost and limits

**View → Ethnicities**, hotkey9, uses exact ethnic identities for palette,
selection, highlight, borders and labels. The atlas has a distinct Ethnicities
directory, founding context, parent/descendant links, inherited-tradition chart
and actual town examples. City/province/capital articles expose local identity
and memory; state articles distinguish ruling heritage from residents. Memory
prose uses actual recorded names/dates, not an LLM or fabricated narrative.

All regular updates use existing city/region/contact/transfer/campaign events
and fixed-size records. There is no new queue, daily kinship simulation, ancestry
walk, terrain/state/culture/ecoregion census or recurring all-pairs comparison.
Explicit upgrades/validation, actor snapshot copies and on-demand atlas exports
may enumerate actors; renderer exports may enumerate cells. Rare new-group
appends grow the actor registry, like other historical actor births.

These are coarse social estimates, not individual self-identifications, exact
minority censuses, genealogies, actual national law or a calibrated theory of
ethnogenesis. Historical populations need not have understood ethnicity in a
modern national sense. Numerical rates/thresholds remain authored game priors.
[Max Planck's discussion of identity and genetic inference](https://www.eva.mpg.de/de/archaeogenetics/ethics/chapter-3/)
motivates keeping biological and social categories distinct;
[its study of language diversity and common identity](https://www.mpg.de/7920795/jb_2013)
motivates not equating a shared language with a shared people. Neither source
estimates the game's coefficients. See VALIDATION.md for executed evidence and
the separate unresolved long-run performance/realism acceptance criteria.
