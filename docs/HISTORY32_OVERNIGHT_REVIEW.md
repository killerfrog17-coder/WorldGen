## Follow-up causes under investigation

1. Bounded retirement/reassignment of unsupported territorial service, keeping
   recognized sovereignty distinct from actual administration.
2. Returning imperial fleets need institutions, stores, repair workshops and
   trained administrative practice, not only ocean wayfinding inherited from
   oral seafarers. Independent household/island settlement must remain possible.
3. Religion needs live carriers, inherited legal/endowment continuity and
   enduring place-bound forms, rather than every supported local rite becoming
   a portable or missionary stage. Identical generic theology is not adequate.
4. Origins, city survival, inland diffusion and the technical bottleneck need
   causal opportunity records, not merely age labels or a global speed multiplier.
