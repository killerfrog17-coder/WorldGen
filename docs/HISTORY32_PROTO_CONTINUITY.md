## Repair and exclusions

`LivingInstitutionalContinuity` recognizes the existing qualification record
only for revision21 or later, while the town remains inhabited. The generic
unorganized-village population cap and dispersal lottery then cease applying.
This creates no court, writing, teacher, food, cultivars, crop area or people.
It changes neither the physical food-capacity calculation nor its costs.

Actual food/distress retirement still executes before this rule. The separate
unsupported-institution decline/dequalification path still applies. Once
dequalified, the town returns to ordinary village treatment. Ordinary villages
remain ephemeral; this is not a lifespan guarantee or a blanket reduction of
settlement mortality. Court prerequisites, origin hazards, barrier-aware
diffusion and the anti-homeland prior are unchanged.

## Historical interpretation

[Wang et al.'s Jericho study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10542335/)
provides primary isotopic/proteomic evidence of communal stability in a
pre-state settlement. Persistent settlement and monumental construction are
not themselves proof of a bureaucratic state.
[Sandeford's comparative model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5990841/)
relates demographic scale to distinct organizational hierarchies, drawing on
archaeological surveys. Neither paper calibrates a universal village lifespan
or a compulsory number of years from farming to statehood. The repair removes
an internally contradictory classification, not that historical uncertainty.
