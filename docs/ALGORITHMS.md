## Hydrology foundation: worldgen-3.18.0 (outlet/whole-cell rules superseded above)

The owner authorized persistent hydrology, full saved worlds and bundled Earth after 3.17. References below to deferral/no persistent water are historical and superseded. The new order is coarse structure, fine geometry/projection/physics, terrain, erosion, climate, hydrology, complete. Hydrology must not change final elevations or ocean topology: it stores water surfaces, acyclic receivers, catchments, contributing area and two-season discharge separately. Glaciation provenance is retained from erosion. Runoff is driven by simulated precipitation/temperature, including snow storage/melt; lakes require a depression and a water budget, not random painted cells. No lake quota or forced continental breakup is permitted.

Earth is an explicit recipe terrain source, not a second generator. Offline NOAA ETOPO2022 ice-surface relief is selected every tenth sample from the 60-arcsecond source and rounded to physical integer metres. The embedded 2160x1080 grid is nearest-sampled onto the requested mesh, with wrapped longitude and clamped poles. Measured elevations bypass sculpting; downstream climate/drainage remain simulations. Geographic ocean seeds preserve known seawater basins whose straits can fall below the grid resolution, excluding closed inland depressions. Real Earth plate/province data are not supplied; diagnostics are marked procedural. Terrain source and algorithm version participate in determinism. Dataset changes require a new version and source checksum.

Hydrology uses ordered Priority-Flood followed by depression/catchment DAG processing. Equal-level spill corridors belong to the depression to avoid false self-drain cycles. Lake water balance replaces inundated land runoff with direct liquid precipitation and temperature-dependent evaporation, propagates upstream lake outflow once, and supports closed dry/partial basins. Cold-season snow is stored and released in a warmer opposite season; permanently frozen precipitation cannot create open-water lakes. River reaches retain contributing area, Strahler order, both seasonal discharges, mean discharge and hydraulic width (`4.8 * sqrt(max seasonal Q)`, bounded 3–1200 m). This is a coarse hydraulic-geometry approximation, not bankfull measurements.

Missed sub-cell outlets can otherwise flood enormous shallow valleys. A local heuristic marks an `UnresolvedSpillway` when a >=10,000 km2 lake candidate has neither glacial/tectonic support nor 250–700 m confinement. Large measured Earth basins with negative pits (>=200,000 km2) are also retained as enclosed-sea candidates. Their classification is inferred from measured basin geometry, not Earth's procedural diagnostic plates. These virtual outlets do not carve or rescale elevations; they represent unresolved channels and can traverse a higher sampled cell. This is an explicit resolution limitation, not a physical groundwater/dam model. No global lake-area target is fitted.

River presentation uses separately bounded triangle ribbons (8192 reaches/piece), not driver-dependent wide lines. Display width is `12 km + 28 * physical width`, with a capped angular width and subdued-to-richer blue hierarchy; the large visual exaggeration makes rivers readable on a whole-world map. Physical width remains unchanged in Core and inspection. Map ribbons use the same seam clipping as terrain. Lake colors and rendered surface heights use water surfaces, while saved terrain keeps lake-bed elevations. Climate currently precedes hydrology without iterative lake-evaporation feedback; observational climate benchmarking remains deferred.

`WorldFile` is a shared persistence adapter: `WGWRLD01` plus gzip-compressed binary XML serializes the entire World, including meshes, relief, provenance, climate, hydrology and stages. Loading checks version, cell count and full-state hash before committing a new view. Reads/writes are cancellable at stream boundaries and bounded to 256 MB compressed/4 GB decoded. JSON remains an export/legacy-import format. Loading a snapshot does not regenerate it; explicitly reconstructing one still calls WorldPipeline with its complete recipe. UI adapters perform parsing/validation and mesh preparation off the interactive thread and retain the previous completed world if loading/presentation fails.

Read OROGEN_ALIGNMENT.md and the license decision before modifying algorithms. All generation is independently written C# in WorldGen.Core (.NET Standard 2.1). No GPL implementation is translated or shipped.

## Coarse structure and fine terrain

1. Build exactly 20,000 coarse Fibonacci/Delaunay cells with fixed irregularity .75 and the canonical-geometry PRNG stream. Stereographic triangulation uses the MIT DelaunatorSharp dependency; pole closure is counted inside 20,000.
2. Grow connected coarse plates from separated seeds, using weighted fronts and transported directional preference. Assign continents as whole-plate groups with the original area budgets and size variety. Evaluate exactly Recipe.Interconnectedness land/sea assignment candidates on this unchanged plate layout. Select the highest interconnectedness score with a stable earliest-candidate tie. Retain coarse geometry, plate/crust arrays and a coarse-only hash.
3. Build Recipe.Detail fine cells (5,000 to 2,560,000), with Recipe.Irregularity and a separate named geometry stream. Packed sorted edge keys create deterministic edges and ordered adjacency without millions of tree allocations.
4. Sample coarse plate identities through an independently chosen spatial perturbation. The original connected weighted growth reconciles desired fine labels, protects one seed per plate, fills every cell, and rebuilds memberships/areas/adjacency. Continental labels follow the projected plate's crust identity. This keeps every fine plate connected, including at minimum detail. It is an independent connectivity method, not the reference's exact smoothing implementation.
5. Recompute plate physics, connected superplates, merge any superplate enclave surrounded by one neighboring group regardless of crust type, then build boundaries, stress and graph distances on the fine mesh. Enclave merging retains the pre-redesign surrounding-crust reclassification behavior. Parent continental fraction is area-weighted after mixed-crust merges. Euler motion, density/drag, ridge/slab forces and mantle fields use independently selected coefficients. Map2 escape/dead-end collision contexts are inactive.
6. Compute coast/convergence/divergence distances, continental basement, provinces, oriented mountains, forelands, shelves, ridges, trenches/arcs, sphere noise, hotspots and volcanic trails on fine cells. Hotspot geographic seeds are independent of cell count.
7. Resolve the structural ocean topology before postprocessing: flood below-zero cells from every ocean-crust cell, retain stranded continental depressions as land without changing their elevations, then apply blended terrain warp, structural-coast-preserving smoothing, elevation-gated detail, hydraulic/glacial/thermal erosion, ridge sharpening, creep and deterministic continental hypsometry on the fine graph. Erosion uses temporary priority-flood receivers and contributing area; these are discarded. It creates no permanent lakes, river reaches, watershed system or protected inland seas.
8. Compute final ocean connectivity from every below-zero ocean-plate cell, allowing multiple disconnected ocean basins. Preserve disconnected below-zero terrain as land without changing its elevation; persistent hydrology remains deferred. Compute two seasonal climate fields and Koppen classes from that ocean mask and final fine terrain. Fixed advection operands and ocean evaporation are cached; synchronous moisture/shadow buffers are reused. Multiplication/division order is preserved, and the optimization must match the reviewed hashes. Heights are physical integer metres.

WorldPipeline is the only generation route, including reapply/cache reconstruction. Core cancellation checks occur in major loops, graph work and streaming hashing. Dependency triangulation and Array.Sort complete their current call before cancellation is observed; neither runs on Unity's UI thread.

## Whole-plate allocation search (worldgen-3.15.0)

The owner superseded complexes and requested the original generation behavior on 1 October 2026. ContinentalComplexCount, ContinentsPerComplex, explicit membership, cell-grown continent outlines, connection masks, and forced land bridges/straits are removed. ContinentCount remains an allocation target as before, distinct from final connected landmass count. Land coverage supports the original 0–1 range; plate granularity and subsequent terrain make achieved land area approximate.

Interconnectedness is an integer search budget from 1 to 50, not a connection-strength slider. Exactly that many whole-plate land/sea assignments are built with deterministic candidate seeds. The score rewards narrow contacts and near contacts between distinct continent identities, penalizing broad shared borders that merge them into one apparent body. Selection does not carve channels, grow extra continent polygons, alter plate geometry, or rerun terrain and erosion for each candidate. It is a coarse allocation heuristic, not a guarantee of final coastline topology or Earth resemblance.

For each pair, contact width is the actual shared border length divided by the square root of the smaller body's area. Width <=.35 earns `1000*(1-width)`; wider contact costs `5000*min(3,width-.35)`. Separated pairs earn `600*max(0,1-gap/1500000)`, with gap in metres measured on the fixed 20,000-cell shoreline graph using nearest-land multi-source distances and frontier-meeting edges, not plate-width estimates. Area-target error costs `10000*abs(land-target)/planetArea`. The total is multiplied by 1000 and rounded to an integer for deterministic comparison.

Candidate zero preserves the original default allocation path. Additional candidates use named independent random streams; increasing the count extends the same sequence and cannot reduce the selected score. Stable iteration order resolves score ties. Candidate count, selected candidate and score are diagnostic outputs. One candidate is the baseline-restoration comparison; new recipe/version fields necessarily change recipe-dependent hash chains. World types and their constraints are deferred; no assignment is rejected for a world-type condition.

The historical [CONTINENT_COMPLEX_REQUIREMENTS.md](CONTINENT_COMPLEX_REQUIREMENTS.md) is retained as an audit record, not an active requirement. Earlier bridge/seaway distances and complex composition acceptance criteria no longer apply.

## Tectonic morphology (worldgen-3.16.0)

### Plate-shape correction (worldgen-3.17.0)

Separated seeds, the per-plate area scheduler and adjacent-cell-only claims remain. The old accumulated near-isotropic travel fronts mostly inflated circles around those seeds; a linear directional bias displaced the circles rather than eliminating radial growth. Named `plate-fabric` streams now supply curved transported axes and plate-scale coherent resistance. Entry costs penalize transverse travel and are quantized to positive integer metres. Frontier priority is `max(previousPriority, 6 * entryCost) + max(1, 22 * entryCost / 100)`: retaining barriers makes growth follow irregular weak zones, while accumulated positive travel limits long corridors. This is a procedural partition heuristic, not a time-stepped lithosphere simulation. All ties remain ordered and all plates remain connected. Fine projection and whole-plate continent allocation are unchanged; CoarseHash still ignores Detail/Irregularity but intentionally differs from 3.16.

Fine ocean shelves now have independently varying shelf/slope widths, with narrower shelves near active margins; arc widths/offsets and back-arc offsets vary coherently instead of drawing constant-distance concentric bands. Modest abyssal relief breaks uniform contours without altering plate identities. Maximum contributing feature width is retained in feature metadata. The 3.16 side-ownership and tectonic-mechanism restrictions below remain in effect.

The allocation search above is unchanged. Fine relief now uses independently designed process-specific profiles informed by the [Earth analogue research](TECTONIC_REALISM.md), not geographic stamps or translated reference code.

Active boundary normals are smoothed over connected same-parent-pair interfaces within 250 km. Oceanic crust goes beneath continental crust; ocean-ocean polarity uses area-weighted parent density and stable identity ties, not conflicting member-plate densities. Connected same-regime/same-polarity belts retain IDs, along-belt and endpoint distances, and explicit overriding-side provenance.

Separate bounded graph fields propagate collision, upper-plate subduction, lower-plate trenches, spreading, oblique compression and inherited relief. Propagation cannot cross the owning parent. Collision combines a narrow mountain front with a broader hinterland plateau and a separate foreland. Ocean-continent margins produce asymmetric cordilleras and occasional seeded inland inherited ranges. Compression plus shear can raise an oblique orogen; pure transform motion cannot. Ocean arcs use narrower offset envelopes, coherent variation, peaks and endpoint taper, with eligible back-arc depressions. Shallow accretion and relict coastal arcs can emerge; no arbitrary continent-centre bridge is drawn.

Hotspots use discrete centres carried in the plate-motion direction, declining relief and increasing synthetic age. Ridge overlap creates a localized broader volcanic island/swell. Ordinary ridge uplift is not an island-retention exception. Inherited ages are seeded proxies, not a simulated tectonic history. Features record owner parent, belt and appreciable contributing cells; cell inspection exposes these mechanisms.

Erosion retains only intentional ocean-island relief with correct owner membership, including shallow accretion. High fold/plateau terrain receives weaker hypsometric remapping to preserve structural cross-sections. Temporary drainage and final ocean connectivity remain unchanged in purpose; no persistent hydrology is restored. All changed outputs are versioned and require explicitly reviewed golden updates.

## Presentation

SurfaceMesh emits Voronoi-style cell fans using adjacent triangle centers. Terrain, Satellite, Climate and categorical layers have constant color per cell. Heightmap interpolates physical heights at centers/corners. This fixes the former blurry center-to-center color interpolation. No higher-detail display mesh exists: Detail belongs to generation.

Shared geometry is yielded in bounded pieces of at most 8,192 dual edges. Wireframe follows dual cell edges. Plate outlines appear on all layers; plate colors replace Terrain colors only. Geographic grids accept Off, 15 or 30 degrees. Map projection unwraps/clips the longitude seam and fills pole strips; every fan has consistent outward orientation before projection.

Unity runs Core and shared presentation in a worker task. A bounded queue holds at most two prepared upload pieces. Update uploads pieces within a per-frame budget; no giant MeshCollider or synchronous normals rebuild occurs. A new root becomes visible only when all pieces succeed. Cancellation keeps the previous complete world/view and releases temporary meshes. Picking intersects the analytic sphere or map plane and queries the core graph.

Map center longitude translates cached geometry and its shared-mesh wrap copies; the shader clips the fixed map rectangle. Longitude dragging performs no cell loops or mesh reconstruction. Globe orientation composes yaw with clamped pitch and never accumulates roll. Zoom scales the distance above the globe surface exponentially.

The browser retains authoritative state in its .NET worker. Its UI receives a small summary, with C# mesh pieces streamed separately; JavaScript only transports data and renders it. New views upload one bounded piece per animation frame. Cancel terminates generation workers or interrupts a presentation request between pieces, retaining the preceding rendered view.
