## Actual limiting skill, not a missing maritime package

The observer's Medieval bundle needs installed steel, mills and scholarly
institutions; one scholarly knowledge level can satisfy the latter. The active
technical graph instead requires scholarly **level3** for printing and
**level4 plus printing2** for experimental science. Early Modern requires
printing/science plus two of gunpowder, ocean navigation and crop rotation.

V21 Earth culture191, Lunusum, retains this actual sequence:

- Ocean navigation installed160447; crop rotation installed161604.
- Printing installed163961; gunpowder installed164155.
- Scholarly level4 acquired164511.
- Experimental science acquired164647, installed164684: the final package gate.

Thus its final delay is not a need to discover seafaring, pay for another crop
package, or wait centuries for final science deployment. Only173 years separate
the recorded scholarly4 acquisition from the installed Early Modern bundle.
V21 CP culture40 likewise has scholarly3 since164127, scholarly4 at165103,
science at165255 and its installation at165274:171 years after scholarly4.

The earlier learning/research chain is long. V21 Earth culture149 has scholarly2
at162462, scholarly3 at163536, and scholarly4 at164552. Culture6 has scholarly1
at160992 and scholarly2 only162386. These are retained actual level dates, not
calendar thresholds; `LevelYear` does not preserve every previous acquisition
after later levels replace it.

A narrower receiver effect is also observable. At163600 culture191 has177661
supported urban residents and scholarly2 progress1896.899403/1960.2, about96.8%

experiments no longer receive work. This can make a particular late recipient
finish via slower instruction rather than its nearly completed experiments.
It does **not** explain the delay before the world's first level3 offer existed,
and the snapshots alone do not establish its total causal time penalty.

Research budgets above are per actual review, not annual amounts. Saved rows
do not contain the full past political/disease/war/site-sampling demand inputs,
so those are not reconstructed from present stocks. Offer dates identify saved
deliveries, not proof that every old sender remains a currently usable route.

## Causal boundary for a later proposal

The strongest demonstrated long chain is the generic scholarly1→3→4 sequence
before broad printing/science, financed from conserved pre-printing research
effort. Final science deployment is comparatively short, real contacts are
present and nonzero scholarly research is visible. Printing and installed
science subsequently multiply total effort in the existing model, explaining
the much faster later acceleration without a calendar unlock.

Any follow-up should first test the intended meaning of those generic skill
ranks and the receiver's mutually exclusive learning/research choice against
controlled actual actors. These findings do not authorize a blanket cost cut,
guaranteed era date, free contact knowledge, or removal of material deployment.
