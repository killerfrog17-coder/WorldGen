## Prospective experiment

Only a culture's first unparented farmstead proposal gets a continuous hazard
multiplier. For immature marginal fields this is `(yield/0.10)^4`, capped at1;
the physical0.02 floor remains. Comfortable fields and the preceding10,000-year
practice exception retain the original hazard. The multiplier belongs inside
the elapsed-time exponential, not as a cadence-dependent final coin flip.

At the recorded0.098367 this multiplier is about0.936; at0.03 it is0.0081.
Using the original600-year base hazard and investment1, the latter implies a
rough74,000-year expected proposal wait, NOT a guaranteed founding date.
Every proposal still needs the existing real population, farming, cold/frozen
habitat, land availability, spacing and provisioning gates. No field clearing
is granted: the initial village continues to bear the virgin landscape cost.
No parented secondary settlement or overseas port gets this relaxed field
eligibility. No new random stream or extra random draw is introduced.

A village is not a proto-city: the independent1200-resident, sustained food,
coordination and qualification requirements remain, as do ecological/homeland
origin priors, physical diffusion and actual distress/abandonment. A marginal
village may fail. The experiment must not be judged by demanding that this
particular Indian community inevitably found a civilization.
